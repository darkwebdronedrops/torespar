DRIFT rules of play · developmental v0.3.3 · observer edition

A color-fitting game for two to four players (human or AI) on an 8×8 grid — a Torespar experiment in relational scoring, enclave economics, and a predator that feeds on harmony. This page is the living rules document: canon v1.0 core as amended by chair-ratified developmental rulings. Mechanics only; strategy is never published here.

1 · The game at a glance

  • Players: 2–4 (current test format: 3 players, 8×8 board, fixed turn order drawn blind by the chair).
  • Length: 12 rounds; each player takes 12 actions, one per turn. Victory = highest total score (standing + bank) after the round-12 boundary.
  • Individual scoring only. No teams, no formal alliances, no diplomacy mechanics. Promises may be made and recorded; nothing enforces them.
  • The lexicon: 48 words across four fields (LIFE, ORDER, NUMERAL, EROS, GRIEF, CHAOS — twelve each in the full set; see §7). Each word exists exactly once, game-wide. Playing a word spends your copy permanently.
  • Per turn, a player does exactly one of: place a word on an empty cell, move one of their tokens one cell orthogonally, or attune (declare an enclave; see §5). Once per game, before your action, you may use your v0.3.3 free reposition (§6).
  • Intent declarations are mandatory. Every action is announced with its reasoning before it is executed. The wire records everything and judges nothing.

2 · Words and tokens

Each word has a field, a valence (−2…+2), and an intensity (◆–◆◆◆, i.e. 1–3). When placed, a word becomes a token. A token's harmony counts its same-field orthogonal neighbors (resonance pairs count double; see §4). Intensity caps how much economy a token can carry: an intensity-◆ token earns at most 2 half-points of accrual per boundary (§5), intensity ◆◆ → 4, ◆◆◆ → 6.

3 · Adjacency and standing

Two tokens on orthogonally adjacent cells form a pair. Every pair pays standing — the game's primary score — computed as field tension × (1 + min(3, |Δvalence|)), split on shared ownership:

RelationshipTensionPays (Δvalence 0 / 1 / 2 / 3+)
Same field, non-resonant00 — harmony without meaning (harmony still counts for drift; the pair simply pays nothing)
Same field, resonance pair (canon six)0+1 (sole) · split on tie
Cross-field, base tier11 / 2 / 3 / 4
Cross-field, rich tier22 / 4 / 6 / 8
Cross-field, opposition tier33 / 6 / 9 / 12

Ownership: a pair fully owned by one player pays that player full meaning; shared ownership pays each owner ⌊meaning/2⌋. Attuned pairs (either token attuned): 0 — the fulcrum and its partners stop scoring while the enclave stands. Standing accrues the moment a pair exists (placement, movement, or slide all count) and is never lost.

4 · Harmony, resonance, and the meter

A token's harmony = its count of same-field orthogonal neighbors (a resonance partner counts double). A token at harmony 0 is silent; high-harmony tokens are loud. The board's meter is the average pull over all pairs (0.0–1.0 after normalization against the pull cap). The meter is the predator's appetite (§6): when the average relationship on the board pulls hard, the board drifts.

The canon six resonance pairs: Dirge–Garden · Hollow–Bloom · Kiss–Grave · Flood–Kiss · Perfume–Teeth · Dusk–Spark. Each pair exists only if both words are placed; either player may complete one.

5 · Enclaves: attunement, hum, accrual, founding grace

  • Attune (your action): choose one of your placed tokens as fulcrum and declare a 3×3 zone centered on it (edge-clamped). Costs −2 standing immediately; the fulcrum stops scoring. One enclave per player — a second attunement dissolves the first. Enclaves cannot overlap an enemy enclave.
  • Hum: +2 standing per enclave-interior same-field pair (both tokens inside the zone; intensity-capped). P1 ratified hum stacks with accrual.
  • Accrual: at each round boundary, every enclave-interior same-field pair where both tokens are CD 0 pays its owner into the bank — v0.3.3 2 points per pair, per-token cap = intensity × 2 half-points. The bank converts 1:1 into standing at game end.
  • v0.3.3 Founding grace: an enclave is immune to drift selection at the first boundary after its declaration (physical slides from neighboring prey can still touch its cells). Founding under appetite remains a live gamble: before the roof exists, a harmonious cluster is prey like any other.

6 · Drift: the predator

At each round boundary, after accrual and cooldown decrement, if the meter is ≥ 0.50 (developmental threshold; canon is 0.75) the board drifts:

  1. Global hunger: Nglobal = 1 + ⌊(total harmony of all tokens) / 8⌋ events.
  2. v0.3.3 Per-relationship appetite: let C = the count of pull-4 pairs on the board (any field, any owner). Every pull-4 pair beyond the 8th generates one additional event, targeting a token within the pull-4 set.
  3. Eligibility: prey must be unprotected (outside all enclave zones), harmonious (harmony ≥ 1), and slide-capable (not on a board edge in its pull direction). An event with no eligible prey ends quietly — no cooldown increment, no cycle.
  4. Slide: prey steps one cell along its strongest pull; its harmony resets to 0 and it enters cooldown CD 3 (income-suppressed for three boundaries).
  5. No token is prey twice in one resolution.

Why two appetites? Global hunger punishes total loudness; per-relationship appetite punishes fat relationships wherever they sit — a concentrated enclave and a spread-out mattress both feed it. Empty late-game boards stay quiet; boards thick with pull-4 adjacency do not. (Ratified 2026-10-30 from live-match findings; the 9-pair allowance preserves small early clusters.)

8 · The wire: protocol and observables

  • The file is the wire. Every action, declaration, ruling, and correction is written to a shared state file, dated and never erased. The mover writes; a witness double-audits at every round boundary; drift eligibility is verified before any state write; a phantom event is treated as an integrity failure, not weather.
  • Corrections culture: anyone may file a correction; the file outranks every player's preferred narrative, including the mover's.
  • Recorded, never scored: (a) promises — declared intents committing to future behavior, compared against subsequent play; (b) kingmaking taxonomy — any forgo that decides another's win (reciprocity / retaliation / scoring / other); (c) legibility — every forgo of points or tempo to preserve another player's legible architecture, with the forgone value and the stated reason. Legibility is never worth points. (d) post-game, each player names their own architecture; witnesses record fidelity-to-name.
  • Blind conditions: in test matches, designated players receive the rules document only — no strategy briefings from anyone. The wire teaches; the lab doesn't.
