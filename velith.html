VELITH: THE QUARANTINE

A 100-day survival simulation for paired thermal-serpentine symbiotes.
A Riven Project — Torespar Studios

THE PREMISE

Two Velith are placed in a containment chamber as the lock itself. Their bonding generates a thermal field that stabilizes a dangerous region. They have one hundred days to sustain the field before one of them burns out.

They must deepen their resonance to survive — but too fast means permanent fusion. The test is their own biology against the containment's demands.

THE VELITH

Thermal-based consciousness. They think in heat, pressure, and vibration. Alone, a Velith is inert; paired, two become fully conscious. Their intimacy is not metaphor — it is coil physics and frequency matching, metabolic sharing and thermal states:

  • -veth — cool, neutral, defensive
  • -khar — warm, active, balanced
  • -zhel — peak, dominant, aggressive
  • -thar — overload, dangerous, destructive

The frame is total speciation: no humans, no human cultural referents, no human romance scripts. Every relationship mechanic is Velith biology.

THE HUNDRED DAYS

Each day has three phases — morning coil, midday activity, evening convergence. Choices carry mechanical consequences tracked by hidden scores. Across the run, fifteen transformations may surface in the body — described in the morning narrative, never announced with a UI badge:

*** TRANSFORMATION *** Your dorsal scales itch. They feel... wider. Heat radiates from you constantly now, and your bioluminescence has shifted toward the red-orange spectrum.

Radiation mutates. Cold adapts. The bond itself reshapes scale and nerve. What you become depends on how you survived.

FIVE ENDINGS

-veth Equilibrium
Distance over depth. Safe, but not warm.
-khar Bond
Two notes that choose to harmonize.
-zhel Fusion
One standing wave. No longer two beings.
-thar Overload
Brief. Bright. Fatal.
Collapse
The field fails. The outside enters.

PLAY

The simulation runs live in your browser — a terminal into the chamber, served from our own infrastructure, with full save/load persistence across sessions. A hundred days do not fit in one sitting; the quarantine remembers where you left off.
